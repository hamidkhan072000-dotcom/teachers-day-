Every lesson leaves something behind.

Teacher's Day isn't only about thanking teachers for what they taught in the syllabus. It's about the moments that happened around it — the patience, the small jokes, the second chances, the corrections that stuck.

This is a record of some of those moments, put together by the students who were there for them.
